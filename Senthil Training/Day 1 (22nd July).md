### Turing Method by Alan Turing:

- Alan Turing is the father of modern computer.
- He was a great mathematician, theoretical biologist, Physician.
- He used ciper and deciper in the time of World War II.
- He then started the "Turing Test", which means checking the basic things are available in a computer. 
- Eg: Like a man and computer is available, the computer wants to convince the Judge that it is a human. Where the human wants to convince that he is the human.
- Here if the computer convinces the judge as it is human, then it passed the test.
- So whenever someone as asks the developement is Turing complete, it means that the code has the fundamental things to do any basic kind of computations.
